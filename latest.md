**Long‑term Significance** – If the moratorium holds, the rapid scaling race that has driven model capability gains over the past five years could be throttled, reshaping the competitive landscape. In 1‑5 years we may see a bifurcation: a handful of “safety‑first” platforms that dominate regulated markets, and a parallel underground or foreign effort to push ever‑larger models outside the agreed envelope. The decision also sets a precedent for industry‑wide self‑regulation, potentially easing the pressure for heavy‑handed government bans while still addressing existential‑risk concerns.
