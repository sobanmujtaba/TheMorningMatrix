**Long‑term Significance (1‑5 years)**  
1. **Policy precedent:** This is the first large‑scale, technology‑provider‑driven conditional service model aimed at shaping K‑12 curricula. If successful, other platforms (Microsoft, Adobe, etc.) could adopt similar “AI‑access‑or‑lose” clauses, fundamentally redefining the relationship between public education and private tech.  
2. **Equity risk:** Schools in under‑funded districts that lack robust AI‑training programs may lose a critical search resource, widening the digital‑divide.  
3. **Legal landscape:** The strategy will likely trigger First‑Amendment and antitrust challenges, setting court precedents for how far a private company can dictate educational content.
