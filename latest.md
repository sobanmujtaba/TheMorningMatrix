## 1. Lead Story  
**Headline & Summary**  
*The Surprising Reasons China Is Skeptical of A.I. Safety Calls* – Beijing’s tech policy circles are openly questioning the “AI safety” narrative advanced by U.S. firms and Western regulators.  Officials point to a mix of geopolitical mistrust, divergent risk assessments, and a belief that Western safety frameworks are designed to curb Chinese competitive advantage rather than mitigate genuine existential threats.  The article notes that Chinese research institutes are pivoting toward “controlled openness,” emphasizing internal standards and limited cross‑border data sharing while continuing to invest heavily in frontier AI models.  

**Long‑term Significance (1‑5 years)**  
- **Regulatory Divergence:** Expect a split between “hard‑line” safety regimes in the U.S./EU and a more “risk‑tolerant” but self‑governed approach in China, complicating global AI product roll‑outs.  
- **Tech Decoupling:** Divergent safety standards could become a de‑facto barrier to technology transfer, accelerating a bifurcated AI ecosystem.  
- **Strategic Leverage:** China may use its skeptical stance to argue for “sovereign AI” standards at multilateral forums, shaping future WTO‑style AI trade rules.

Today’s article shows the skepticism has hardened, shifting from cooperative dialogue to a defensive posture that will influence upcoming G20 AI discussions slated for early 2027.
