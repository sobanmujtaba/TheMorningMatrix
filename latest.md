## 1. Lead Story  
**Headline & Summary** – *Alphabet’s Q3 revenue miss reignites AI‑capex skepticism as Wall Street eyes a looming pull‑back.*  
Alphabet reported Q3 revenue of **$73.2 bn**, missing consensus by **2 %** and flagging slower growth in its AI‑driven ad products. The miss sent the stock down **7 %** in pre‑market trade and revived concerns that the massive AI‑focused data‑center build‑out may be over‑leveraged. 3. **Investor Sentiment** – Persistent earnings volatility may re‑price the “AI premium” on big‑tech stocks, influencing index weighting and fund allocations.  
4. **Regulatory Scrutiny** – A pull‑back may ease pressure from antitrust bodies concerned about AI‑driven market consolidation, but could also prompt new inquiries into AI‑related expenditures.

Together, these threads suggest we are at a **pivot point** where the industry must reconcile rapid AI advancement with sustainable financing.
