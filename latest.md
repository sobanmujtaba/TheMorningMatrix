## 1. Lead Story  
**Headline & Summary**  
*AI chatbots are becoming political advisers. Their answers can mislead voters* – NPR reports that sophisticated language models are now being marketed and used as “political counsel” tools for campaign staff, independent voters, and even elected officials. The article documents several pilot programs in swing states where chatbots field policy questions, generate talking points, and simulate constituency feedback. Critics warn that the models, trained on partisan data and lacking transparent provenance, can subtly bias answers, amplify misinformation, and erode public trust in democratic deliberation.  

**Long‑term Significance (1‑5 years)**

- **Regulatory pressure:** Within three years, the Federal Election Commission and the FTC are likely to draft disclosure rules mandating “AI‑generated content” labeling for any political communication, similar to the EU’s AI Act.  
- **Electoral outcomes:** Mis‑aligned or manipulated chatbot outputs could swing close races, prompting post‑election audits of AI usage—a new frontier for election‑security law.
