# The Morning Matrix — 2026‑10‑25  

## 1. Lead Story  
**Headline & Summary** – *“No Signs of AI Payoff Outside the Tech Sector”* – A report from Apollo Global Management’s Daily Spark finds that, despite a year‑long hype cycle, tangible productivity gains from generative AI remain confined to software, cloud services, and semiconductor firms. Companies in manufacturing, retail, and logistics have yet to demonstrate measurable cost‑savings or revenue uplift, and many pilot projects are stalled by data‑quality, talent, and regulatory hurdles.  

**Long‑term Significance (1‑5 yr)** – If the trend persists, the AI‑driven productivity surge that investors have priced into S‑P 500 earnings could be overstated, leading to a market correction and a slowdown in capital allocation to AI‑centric startups. Conversely, the lag may incentiv governments and industry groups to launch coordinated “AI‑adoption accelerators,” potentially reshaping workforce training, data‑infrastructure policy, and cross‑sector standards by 2029‑30.
