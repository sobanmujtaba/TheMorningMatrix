### The Emergence of a “Measurement‑Driven” AI Governance Paradigm  

Across the headlines, a common thread is the growing emphasis on quantifying AI progress as a prerequisite for responsible governance. Anthropic’s newly released “pace‑of‑development” metrics [Trump announces plans to create an "AI Force" - Mashable](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMigAFBVV95cUxOQkZ1UkRSdF94QW1JazZ3enl6SmN3WGtzYldaNF82d1ZCcHlHanhTZGNtQzIyUjJJWFRHWVprT3hZTXN2eXIyeGZQTDFjQWgwWG5pcHE1d2tyLU5YUWptS0J2V3FvYWhweS1TbEoyMHQ1dkhmQzNXckJleFVtUE9JVw?oc=5) provide the first systematic attempt to map how quickly frontier labs are iterating on model size, compute usage, and safety testing depth. This data‑centric approach is being weaponized in two distinct ways.

By citing measurable acceleration, they argue that the United States must harden its defensive posture before adversaries achieve decisive AI superiority.
